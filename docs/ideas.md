# Ideas from playtests

Ideas that came out of playing builds but are not scheduled yet. Each line says who raised it and when. When an idea is picked up, move it into `requirements.md` or the design doc and strike it here.

| Idea | Raised | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Limited horse boost to catch up with a train | Devlin, M3 playtest, 2026-10-09 | A short burst above top speed with a cooldown or a stamina cost. It fits the `handlingModel` variant group (stamina, speed tiers) that the design already reserves. |
| Resistance while boarding: guards on the train shoot at the rider, perhaps only at higher heat | Devlin, M3 playtest, 2026-10-09 | Needs combat (phase 3) and heat (phase 2). It could be a heat threshold in config, so heat makes the approach harder as well as the run. |
| More track layouts and terrain, to see whether runs vary enough to stay fun | Devlin, M3 playtest, 2026-10-09 | The map pipeline already supports several routes per map, speed zones and terrain zones; this needs new maps drawn in Tiled. |
| Moving between cars and onto the roof | Devlin, M3 playtest, 2026-10-09 | In the requirements (traversal: the roof bypasses a car). Not in any phase's build list yet; multi-car interiors were deferred to phase 2. |
