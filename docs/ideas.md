# Ideas

The ideas list now lives on the project wiki: [Ideas](https://github.com/devlinjunker/train-robber/wiki/Ideas).

It indexes every idea that is written down but not scheduled in a phase yet, including ideas raised in playtests. Add new ideas there with who raised them and when. When an idea is scheduled, move it into [requirements.md](requirements.md) or [technical-design.md](technical-design.md) and strike it on the wiki.
